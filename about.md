# Rod Ball Selector

Choose how Pulse Rods look in Geometry Dash!

Rod Ball Selector lets you choose one of four appearance options for each level:

- **Normal** — use the game's normal Rod appearance.
- **Rod 1** — use Rod variant 1.
- **Rod 2** — use Rod variant 2.
- **Rod 3** — use Rod variant 3.

## How to use

1. Open a level information page.
2. Press the Rod Ball Selector button near the top-right of the page.
3. Select the appearance you want.
4. Play the level.

Your choice is saved per level. A level without a saved choice starts with **Normal** selected.

## Requirements

- Geode
- Node IDs
